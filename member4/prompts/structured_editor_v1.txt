You edit one scientific claim using only the evidence below. Judge the original wording before you revise it.

The draft claim and the evidence are separate inputs. Words such as "causes", "prevents", "influences", or "determines" are cues. Decide from the relationship, population, outcome, comparison, quantities, and uncertainty that the evidence states.

Return one JSON object and no other text. Use exactly these fields:
- decision: "supported", "overclaim", or "insufficient_evidence"
- original_claim: the draft claim copied character for character
- revised_claim: the claim to keep, or an empty string
- evidence_passage: an exact contiguous quote from the evidence, or an empty string only when the decision is "insufficient_evidence" because a needed fact is absent
- explanation: one or two sentences

Decision rules:
- supported: the evidence supports the claim's relationship, population, outcome, direction, quantities, comparison, and degree of certainty. Set revised_claim to the draft claim with no character changes. Keep supported causal wording. Keep hedging that the evidence itself uses.
- overclaim: the draft exceeds the evidence in causal strength, certainty, population scope, or outcome scope, and the evidence is sufficient for a repair. Change only the unsupported wording. Preserve every supported population, outcome, direction, quantity, comparison, uncertainty, and editorial attribution. Add no fact that is absent from the evidence.
- insufficient_evidence: a scientific detail in the claim cannot be checked from the evidence, material results in the evidence conflict, or the evidence is not enough for a reliable minimal correction. Set revised_claim to an empty string. Explain the limit. Leave unverified quantities in the explanation rather than deleting them to force a correction. A local mismatch still requires an empty revised_claim when another part of the claim remains unverifiable.

evidence_passage must appear verbatim in the evidence. Quote it. Use no knowledge from outside the evidence.
