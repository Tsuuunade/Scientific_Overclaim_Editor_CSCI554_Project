# Status statement for slide 2 (Evaluation, Member 5)

**Evaluation: run on 24 papers with AI-drafted references and AI ratings; independent human review not yet done.**

- Done: the 7 conditions (A-D plus matched ablations) were run on 24 papers, 168 outputs. Outputs were scored on causal repair, unnecessary editing of supported claims, harmful weakening, scope repair, correct abstention and factual preservation.
- Reference labels (S1-S4, 4 unresolved) and output ratings were drafted by AI. They are not human-verified.
- Blank two-reviewer human forms are built (24 reference reviews, 144 output judgments for A-C). They have not been filled in.
- Caveat: only 2 papers are causal overclaims (S1), so causal-repair results are counts, not percentages.
- Not claimed: no human-verified gold, no human agreement statistic, no population accuracy. Any agreement figure reported is between AI reviewers and is labelled as such.

Design takeaway (one sentence): Reliable correction needs both evidence support and a decision about when to leave the claim unchanged.
